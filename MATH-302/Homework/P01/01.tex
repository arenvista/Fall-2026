\section{Question 1}
Show that every normed vector space $\left(V, \left\| \cdot \right\| \right)$ is automatically a metric space $(V,d)$ with the metric $d$ given by $d(x,y) = \left\| x-y \right\|$

We demonstrate all principles of a metric; that is for all $x,y,z \in V$ we have:

\begin{cbox}{catblue}{Metric Space Properties}
	adsa
	\begin{table}[h]
		\centering
		\begin{tabular}{|l|l|}
			\hline
			Property                    & Description                  \\
			\hline
			Identity of Indiscernibles: & $d(x,y) = 0 \iff x=y$        \\
			\hline
			Symmetry                    & $d(x,y) = d(y,x)$            \\
			\hline
			Triangle Inequality         & $d(x,y) \le d(x,z) + d(z,y)$ \\
			\hline
			Non-Negatitivity            & $d(x,y) \ge 0$               \\
			\hline
		\end{tabular}
	\end{table}
	sdasd
\end{cbox}
